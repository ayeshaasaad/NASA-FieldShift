NASA data we use

  • GWETROOT: root-zone soil wetness (NASA POWER, built with satellite data including SMAP)
  • PRECTOTCORR: daily rainfall (NASA POWER)
  • T2M_MAX: daily maximum temperature (NASA POWER)
  • Open-Meteo: 3-day rain forecast (not NASA data)

Limits

  • NASA data gives estimates for an area, not an individual field.
  • We flag conditions linked to salt risk; we don't measure salt.
  • Our rules are simple and for exploring options, not final advice. Check with a local agriculture expert.

Data: NASA POWER (power.larc.nasa.gov) · Forecast: Open-Meteo · NASA does not endorse this project.
